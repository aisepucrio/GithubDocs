# Data licensing status

The rights holders' choice of an open license for the original evaluation
measurements and annotations is pending. CC BY 4.0 has been proposed; it has
**not** been applied by this notice. No additional rights are granted by the
software license to the contents of `data/`, `reference/` or generated artifacts.

The compressed exports also contain third-party source/documentation excerpts,
rendered prompts and model outputs. Those portions must retain applicable
third-party rights and notices and must not be indiscriminately relicensed as
original research data. Resolve this distinction before publishing the archive.
